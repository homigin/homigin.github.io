Ch.23 Facility Design, Layout, and Organization

Study notes for Ch.23 Facility Design, Layout, and Organization from Essentials of Strength Training and Conditioning.

Learning objectives#

  • identify the aspects of new facility design, including the four phases (predesign, design, construction, and preoperation);
  • identify the aspects of modification of an existing facility, along with the differences between design of a new facility and modification or renovation of an existing facility;
  • explain how to assess an athletic program’s needs in order to design a facility that is well suited to these needs;
  • explain how to design specific facility features, including supervision location, access, ceiling height, flooring, environmental factors, electrical service, and mirrors;
  • explain how to arrange equipment in organized groups, creating better traffic flow throughout the facility; and
  • explain the maintenance and cleaning needs for the surfaces and equipment in a strength and conditioning facility.

General Aspects of New Facility Design#

  • Predesign phase: Needs analysis, feasibility study and master plan formation -> bid architect
  • Needs analysis: How much space? What need to fit?
  • Feasibility: SWOT analysis. 經濟考量.
  • Master plan: 後續規劃大方向。 施工,設計,預算,營運(短期 長期目標)
  • Design phase: 符合法規下最佳動線
  • 設計委員會需要包括 S&C
  • Traffic flow
  • For ease of access, and to create a space with a clear view in the middle of the room, low machines and dumbbell racks can be placed in the center

Assessing Athletic Program Needs#

  • How many athletes?
  • Training goals?
  • Demographics? Training experience?
  • Scheduling
  • The recommended minimum amount of space per athlete is 100 square feet (9.3 m²) per participant.

Designing the Strength and Conditioning Facility#

  • Location: 盡量在一樓. 不要吵到別人. If not 一樓, 樓地板支撐 >100 lb/ ft² (488 kg/m²)

  • Supervision: 要在中間 可以看到大家，可以略高

  • Access: 落差>0.5in (13mm) 就要有坡道. 坡道斜度：↑ 1 in -> 12 inch (1:12) 門要大

  • Ceiling: at least 12-14 ft. (3.6-4.2m)

  • Floor: 橡膠 防黴地毯 人工草皮. 舉重區 中間木頭+橡膠周邊

  • 燈光： between 50 and 100 lumens,

溫度：72°F to 78 °F (22-25 °C) 濕度：不超過60% 空氣：HVAC 空氣一小時換8-12次. 聲音：< 90 分貝

  • Mirror: at least 6 inches (15 cm) away from any equipment and a minimum of 20 inches(51 cm) above the floor.

weight plate: 18 inches wide

Arranging equipment#

  • Barbells and dumbbells should have a minimum of 36 inches (91 cm) of space between other barbells and dumbbells to allow for movement between racks without danger to the lifter or spotter(s).
  • walkways at least 36 in wide
  • at least 49 square feet (4.6 m²) of open space so that athletes can perform a dynamic warm-up
  • Circuit training
  • circuit training machines should be at least 24 inches (61 cm)—preferably 36 inches (91 cm)—away from each other in order to provide a wide enough walk space and some extra space for safety called a safety cushion.
  • Any designated walkways in the circuit training area should be between 4 and 7 feet (1.2 and 2.1 m) wide
  • Racks and platforms should be spaced 3 to 4 feet (0.9 to 1.2 m) apart.
  • weightlifting racks should be bolted to the floor
