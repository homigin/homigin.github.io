Learning objectives#

  • identify the members of the sports medicine team and their responsibilities during the rehabilitation and reconditioning of injured athletes,
  • recognize the types of injuries athletes sustain,
  • comprehend the timing and events of tissue healing,
  • understand the goals of each tissue healing phase, and
  • describe the role of the strength and conditioning professional during injury rehabilitation and reconditioning. Essentials

Sports Medicine team#

成員角色
Physician (Team doctor)領導團隊、確定診斷與 rehab 開始時機
Athletic Trainer (ATC)評估傷勢、設計並執行 rehab 計畫,貼紮預防
Physical Therapist (PT)專業治療與功能回復訓練
Strength & Conditioning Coach設計 reconditioning 計畫（傷後體能恢復、重返賽場）
Psychologist or Counselor協助面對傷後心理壓力、恐懼
Nutritionistprovide guidelines regarding proper food choices to optimize tissue recovery.

溝通#

  • 了解 rehab phase & 適合的運動模式
  • 定期更新 progress、pain level、功能狀態
  • 回報任何 exercise-related concern（e.g. 動作代償、疼痛回發）
  • indication & contraindication
  • Types of injury: Macrotrauma (disloation, subluxation, sprain, contusion) vs Microtrauma

Healing Phases & Timeline#

Phase特徵時間範圍Focus
Inflammatoryredness, swelling, pain0–4 days↓ pain & inflammation, passive ROM, isometrics
Fibroblastic Repaircollagen synthesis, scar forming~2 days–6 wkbegin gentle strength, active ROM, low-load isotonics
Maturation-Remodeling↑ collagen strength, ↑ function≥3 wk–數月↑ strength, neuromuscular ctrl, functional return

Closed vs Open Kinetic Chain#

分類特徵範例
CKCdistal segment 固定，↑ joint stabilitysquat, lunge, push-up
OKCdistal segment 自由，isolated mvmtleg extension, biceps curl
  • CKC：↑ proprioception, stability → rehab early-mid stage 使用
  • OKC：isolated strength ↑ → 用於 late stage strength rebuilding

Program design#

  • Resistance Training:
  • Delorme and Oxford method:
  • 3*10 with pyramid design Delorme - 3x10 @ 50% 10RM, 75% 10RM, and 100% 10RM respectively 輕到重 Oxford - 3x10 @ 100% 10RM, 75% 10RM, 50% 10RM 重到輕

daily adjustable progressive resistive exercise (DAPRE)#

  • 4*10 @ 50% 1RM, 6 @ 75% 1RM, max reps @ 100% estimated 1RM
  • Sport-specific movements and training structure
  • Ex: higher reps when rehabbing endurance athletes, lower for strength/power athletes

Reducing Risk of Injury and Reinjury#

  • Previous injury is one of the most substantial risk factors for additional injury
  • Risk factors for upper extremity injury : ↓ shoulder ROM, scapular dyskinesis, and ↓ shoulder strength
  • include ROM exercises and possibly the “Thrower’s Ten”
  • Risk factors for lower extremity injury - ↓ balance, neuromuscular control during jump landing, and lower extremity strength
  • single leg squat, plyometric landings

🎯 High Yield / Take-Home Points#

  • Rehab = team work，S&C ≠ rehab leader，但是關鍵實踐者
  • Rehab phase 明確區分 → phase-specific training 📈
  • Healing ≠ linear progression，須隨時調整
  • 溝通流暢 = 最重要的 injury recovery 預測因子之一
