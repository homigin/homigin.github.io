Learning objectives#

  • evaluate the requirements and characteristics of a sport and assess an athlete for the purpose of designing a resistance training program;
  • select exercises based on type, sport specificity, technique experience, equipment availability, and time availability;
  • determine training frequency based on training status, sport season, load, exercise type, and other concurrent exercise;
  • arrange exercises in a training session according to their type;
  • determine 1-repetition maximum (1RM), predicted 1RM from a multiple RM, and RM loads;
  • assign load and repetitions based on the training goal;
  • know when and by how much an exercise load should be increased;
  • assign training volumes according to the athlete’s training status and the training goal; and
  • determine rest period lengths based on the training goal.

Principles#

specificity#

  • muscles involved, the movement pattern, and the nature of the muscle action
  • SAID : specific adaptation to imposed demands
  • preseason -> in-season -> postseason: generalized -> sport specific

overload :#

比習慣的強度再難一點點

  • 增加 sets, 每週次數, 減少休息 等等

progression#

raising the number of weekly training sessions, adding more drills or exercises to each session, changing the type or technical requirements of the drills or exercises, or otherwise increasing the training stimulus.

1. Needs analysis#

  • 兩大步驟：

Evaluation of the Sport（運動項目需求分析）

分析說明
Movement Analysis動作模式、主要肌群
Physiological Analysis主要需求是力、爆發、肌肥大或肌耐？
Injury Analysis常見傷害部位與成因
  • 🔁 需同時考慮是否有速度、敏捷、心肺等需求，但僅針對阻力訓練設計時，聚焦四個面向：Strength / Power / Hypertrophy / Muscular Endurance

Assessment of the Athlete（運動員個人狀況分析）

  • Training status：the classification exist on a continuum
  • Assess training background: type of program, length, intensity, technique experience

測驗與評估：

  • MMT
  • maximum strength testing
  • 肌力、肌耐、柔軟、速度、爆發力
  • 測驗選擇須對應運動特性與現有器材
  • goals: improve deficiencies, maintain strengths, or further develop physiological qualities
  • 主要訓練目標只能選一個（ex: 肌力 / 爆發力 / 肌肥大 / 耐力），一季專注一項目標為佳

2. Exercise selection#

分類說明範例
Core Exercises多關節、訓練主力肌群、與運動表現直接相關squat, bench, clean
Assistance Exercises單關節、支援性質、矯正動作biceps curl, calf raise
Structural Exercises軀幹需穩定負重squat, overhead press
Power Exercises高速動作 → 爆發力訓練主力power clean, snatch

Movement Analysis of the Sport#

  • Sport-Specific Exercises - specific adaptation to imposed demands (SAID) principle
  • Muscle Balance
  • balance between joints and opposing muscle groups
  • test isokinetic test to see relative strength
  • recovery exercise - light loaded or low intensity aerobic

實務考量因素#

考量說明
動作技術需求新手以機械、輔助為主
器材可用性若無器材，需替代設計（如無 clean → front squat + press）
訓練時間時間少時優先全身性動作
賽季期程休季可增加複雜與爆發力訓練；季中應維持與恢復為主

範例（依運動項目）#

運動員賽季核心動作建議
籃球員PreseasonHang clean, push press, squat
美式足球線鋒Off-seasonPower clean, deadlift, bench press
長跑選手In-seasonLunge, vertical press, 核心穩定訓練

3. Training frequency#

  • 影響因素：

  • 🧠 Training status（初學者需更多休息）

  • 📅 Sport season（季節期程，如賽季、季後等）

  • 🏋️ Exercise type & Load（大重量、複合動作需較多恢復時間）

  • 🔁 Concurrent Training（有無搭配其他訓練如有氧、技能等）

  • 通則：

  • 每組肌群訓練後至少休息 1–3天

  • 初學者常見建議：全身性訓練每週2–3次

  • 進階者可進行 Split Routine，不同部位分天訓練（如下肢 Mon/Thu，上肢 Tue/Fri）

  • 各級訓練頻率建議：

    訓練狀態推薦頻率（次/週）
    初學者2–3
    中等者3–4
    高階者4–7

  • 依運動季節：

    賽季推薦頻率（次/週）
    Off-season4–6
    Preseason3–4
    In-season1–3
    Postseason0–3

4. Exercise order#

  • 原則： ✅ 技術性高 & 易疲勞動作先做 ✅ 依肌群大小 & 關節數安排順序 ✅ 減少疲勞對動作品質的影響

  • 常見安排方法：

Power → Core → Assistance

  • Power（如 snatch, clean）最需專注與爆發 → 須優先執行 Core = 多關節動作（如 bench press, squat） Assistance = 單關節小肌群（如 biceps curl, triceps pushdown）

  • Upper/Lower Body Alternation

this method is also referred to as circuit training —a method sometimes also used to improve cardiorespiratory endurance

  • 交替安排上下肢動作 → 節省時間 & 提高恢復效率 適合時間有限 or 初學者

Push/Pull Alternation

  • 推（如 shoulder press）↔ 拉（如 bent-over row）交替安排，減少局部肌肉疲勞

Superset / Compound Set

  • Superset：對立肌群交互（如 biceps curl ↔ triceps pushdown） Compound set：同肌群不同動作連續做（如 biceps curl → hammer curl）

5. Training load and repetitions#

Mechanical WorkForce × Distance一般用於物理計算，不常應用於訓練設計本身
Volume-loadSets × Reps × Weight✅ 實務中用於估算訓練總量（常見）
Repetition-volumeSets × Reps（不含重量）✅ 用於估算訓練量而不考慮強度（例如期化分析）
Intensity（力學）Work / Time（≠%1RM）❌ 較少應用於阻力訓練強度定義（%1RM 更常用）

💡 負重定義#

  • Load = 每組所舉重量，與目標密切相關
  • 可用 %1RM、RM 測驗或多次RM估算

1RM and Multiple-RM Testing Options#

  • Testing the 1RM: 3RM test could be used
  • 10RM load is secondary option
  • A 1RM testing protocol: warm up 5-10 下 -> 休1 -> +10/30 做3-5下 -> 休2-4m -> +10/30 做2-3下 > 休2-4m -> +10/30 1RM -5/15 pounds
  • 多次 RM for assistance exercise: the heaviest load the assistance exercises should be assigned is an 8RM

90% - 4 85% - 6 80% - 8 75% - 10 67% -12

🎯 訓練目標與建議強度 (%1RM) & 重複次數#

目標%1RMReps備註
Strength≥85%≤6Core lifts
Power (單次爆發)80–90%1–2Ex: snatch
Power (多次爆發)75–85%3–5Ex: hang clean
Hypertrophy67–85%6–12中等重量、高量
Muscular Endurance<67%≥12輕重量、高反覆

  • peak power is generally reached with the lifting of very light loads— from body weight (0%) to 30% of the 1RM

  • 包含 power 訓練時 一週一次heavy day to prevent overtraining. medium day =90% load. light day = 80% load.

🔺 Load Progression 建議：#

  • 2-for-2 Rule：在最後一組能多做2 reps, 連續兩次訓練 → 應增加負重

6. Volume#

定義：#

  • Repetition-volume：總 reps 數（ex: 3組x10 = 30 reps）
  • Volume-load：sets × reps × weight（ex: 2×10×50kg = 1000kg）
  • 高Volume → 高代謝負荷與適應
  • volume-load divided by repetition-volume results in the average weight lifted per repetition, which is related to intensity or the quality of work
  • running (rep) volume: distance. intensity value: pace. 所以跑步volume 用 pace*distance
  • Multiple vs single set
  • performing three sets of 10 repetitions without going to failure enhances strength better than one set to failure in 8 to 12 repetitions

💡 建議 volume 設定（依訓練目標）#

  • 📌 Assistance exercise 通常 1–3 sets 即可
  • power: three to five sets (after warm-up) for power exercise
  • hyper: performing three or more exercises per muscle group is the most effective strategy for increasing muscle size

7. Rest periods#

🕒 休息時間根據 目標 + 重量 + 動作類型 調整#

  • Power → 須完全恢復（神經系統）
  • Hypertrophy → 中短休，促進代謝壓力
  • Endurance → 極短休息，增加時間壓力與心肺參與
