Ch.15 Exercise Technique for Free Weight and Machine Training

Study notes for Ch.15 Exercise Technique for Free Weight and Machine Training from Essentials of Strength Training and Conditioning.

Learning objectives#

  • understand the general techniques involved in properly performing resistance training exercises,
  • provide breathing guidelines,
  • determine the appropriateness of wearing a weight belt,
  • provide recommendations for spotting free weight exercises, and
  • teach proper resistance training exercise and spotting techniques.

Fundamentals#

Grips#

Grip特徵用途/範例
Pronated (overhand)掌心向下bench press, pull-up
Supinated (underhand)掌心向上chin-up, biceps curl
Alternated一手正握一手反握deadlift
Hook grip拇指夾住 bar 下方Olympic lifts
Neutral grip掌心相對hammer curl
  • 握距分類：寬、中、窄 → 依動作需求決定

Stable Body & Limb Positioning 🧍#

  • 穩定性原則：腳穩定貼地 + barbell line of action ≈ 重心
  • 地面站姿：雙腳 shoulder-width，拇指朝前，膝蓋微彎

椅上臥姿五點接觸（Five-Point Contact）：

  • 後頭部
  • 上背與肩胛
  • 臀部
  • 左腳足底
  • 右腳足底

Range of Motion (ROM) and Speed#

  • 完整 ROM = 最大化肌肉發展與彈性，控制動作 → 避免使用動能（momentum）
  • Speed: Controlled ≠ always slow 通常：eccentric 稍慢，concentric 爆發（但非 ballistic）

Breathing & Valsalva#

  • exhale through the sticking point

  • Exhale during concentric → esp. sticking point Inhale during eccentric 例：bench press ⏩ exhale halfway ↑、inhale ↓

Valsalva maneuver：

  • transient（1–2 sec）閉氣 ☑️
  • ↑ intra-ab pressure → ↑ spinal support
  • 🚫副作用：HTN, dizziness, blackout
  • 限於 trained lifter + structural lifts（e.g. 1RM squat）

Weight Belt#

  • 使用時機：大重量，腰部負擔大（如 squat, deadlift）
  • 不需使用：小重量或非腰部主力動作

✅ Spotting#

  • 必要情境：bar over head/face/back/shoulder/clavicles

重要原則：

  • Overhead bar on back/front: 要在 power rack. 放保護槓. 要淨空區域.

  • over-face：liftoff 用 alternated grip，lift 中用 supinated grip solid, wide base of support with the feet and a neutral spine position

  • squat：從後方扶住胸椎兩側

  • dumbbell spotting：手靠近手腕，而非肘部 spot as close to the dumbbells as possible

  • Do Not Spot Power Exercises

Lunge / Step-Up#

  • Knee tracking：膝蓋應對齊第二腳趾，避免 valgus

  • Foot plant direction：前腳朝正前方/略內轉（如前所述） Plant the lead foot flat on the floor pointing straight ahead or slightly inward.

  • Step-up platform 高度：膝關節彎曲角約 90°（太高易代償）

  • 📌 RDL

  • 適合：肌力發展、臀腿訓練週期中期

  • 應用：運動員 posterior chain 訓練、減少腰壓

  • Cue：臀部向後推、bar 貼腿滑下、脊椎中立

  • 📌 Stiff-Leg DL 適合：hamstring flexibility 測試、輕負荷輔助訓練

  • 應用：目標拉伸感與 isolation 動作

  • Cue：膝蓋鎖定、髖關節主導、慢控制下放
