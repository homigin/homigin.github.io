Learning objectives#

  • describe the underlying biomechanical constructs of sprint, change-of-direction, and agility performance;
  • apply sound movement principles to the coaching of locomotion modes and techniques;
  • analyze the abilities and skills needed to perform specific movement tasks;
  • effectively monitor the development of sprint, change-of-direction, and agility abilities;
  • apply sound means and methods for developing speed, change of direction, and agility; and
  • design and implement training programs to maximize athletic performance.
  • 定義
  • Speed：直線加速與最大速度（linear sprint speed）
  • Agility：包括改變方向（change-of-direction, COD）與反應（perceptual-cognitive abilities）
  • Speed requires the ability to accelerate and reach maximal velocity, whereas agility performance requires the use of perceptual– cognitive ability in combination with the ability to decelerate and then reaccelerate in an intended direction

Speed and Agility Mechanics#

  • froce, 是一個向量(vector)

  • Speed 速率 是一個純量 -> 多快 cover 一段距離 Velocity 速度 是向量 ＝ Speed + direction

  • Rate of force development (RFD) : max force in minimal time; ∆ force/ ∆ time

  • RFD 比 maximum force 有用 因為 the generation of maximal contraction force takes at least 300 ms, while many sport activities consume 0 to 200 ms

  • Impulse : 決定 change of momentum 的大小

  • = force × time（force–time curve 下的面積）

  • ↑ impulse → ↑ momentum 改變（加速、減速、再加速）

  • sprinting 時，stance phase 地面反作用力的施力時間 × 大小決定 impulse

  • braking impulse is the production of braking forces over certain periods of time

Neurophysiological Basis for Speed#

  • Nervous System
  • Strength training enhances neural drive, the rate and amplitude of impulses being sent from the nervous system to the target muscles
  • 神經驅動 (Neural drive)：↑ motor unit recruitment + firing rate → ↑ RFD + muscular contraction speed
  • 🔁 SSC (Stretch-Shortening Cycle)
  • 定義：肌肉先被快速拉長（eccentric），接著立即短縮（concentric）

機轉：

  • 肌肉彈性能量儲存
  • 肌梭刺激 → 增強反射收縮
  • 訓練設計需要符合
  • involve skillful, multijoint movements that transmit forces through the kinetic chain and exploit elastic–reflexive mechanisms.
  • structured around brief work bouts or clusters separated by frequent rest pauses
  • 出現 complex training: plyo + heavy resistance in same session. 利用肌肉的 postactivation potentiation 特性 -> 強度高不適合新手
  • Spring-Mass Model
  • 身體視為「彈簧」+「質量」結構 腿部肌肉與肌腱群視為彈簧 → 儲存/釋放彈性動能 當地面接觸（ground contact）時：軀幹與腿部結構壓縮 → 彈性儲能；彈力回彈驅動離地 → ↑水平與垂直推進力

Leg stiffness（腿部剛性）：決定身體對地面反作用力的回應

  • ↑ stiffness → ↑ 反應速度與爆發力
  • as stride frequency increases at a given running speed, one of the most important features of the leg spring is an increase in muscle stiffness
  • effective braking is an important part of agility performance；neuromuscular development with respect to high-velocity and high-force eccentric contractions should be considered
  • Proprioception + Visual + Vestibular → 共同整合快速調整動作 Agility ≠ COD：Agility = COD + Perception + Reaction

Running Speed - Sprinting#

  • Running speed = stride length × stride rate
  • 關鍵能力：快速施力、短接觸時間、有效 SSC 利用
  • Phases：
  • Flight phase：空中換腳恢復
  • Support phase：接地、產力、推進
  • Sprint speed is determined by an athlete’s stride length and stride rate; more successful sprinters tend to have longer stride lengths as a result of properly directed forces into the ground while also demonstrating a more frequent stride rate. These findings suggest that RFD and proper biomechanics are two of the primary limiting factors influencing sprint performance

Sprinting Technique#

Phase特徵關鍵要素
Startlow body angle, push-off↑ RFD, reaction time
Acceleration↑ posture, ↑ stride length/rate20–30m內完成、↑ propulsive impulse
Top speedupright, max velocity (30–60m)↓ contact time, ↑ stiffness, SSC

  • Start: 前腳90 ° 後腳133 °

  • vertical velocity is high. 中心上移

  • Optimal length of touchdown of first step is 0.5 m from start line

  • 加速

  • recovery of the swing legs should be low to the ground

  • 高手 stride length of 1.13 to 1.15 m during the first two steps 比新手距離短！ (1.2-1.5m) 旭要增加地板接觸時間來提升速度

  • Max velocity

  • Front-side mechanics > Back-side mechanics

  • front：hip flexion, knee lift, dorsiflexion → ↑ vertical force

  • back：hip extension, leg drive → 避免 excessive ROM

Training Gaols#

  • ↑ Stride Length（步幅）
  • ↑ Stride Rate（步頻）
  • ↓ Ground Contact Time（接地時間）
  • ↑ RFD & Impulse（爆發力與力時積）
  • ↑ eccentric strength → better braking control
  • ↑ reactive strength → better SSC utilization

Agility Performance and Change-of-Direction Ability#

  • 關鍵力學元素：
  • Braking impulse（有效減速）
  • Propulsive impulse（再加速力）
  • Body lean angle（決定轉向效率）
  • Foot placement：腳落地點應在 COG 下方，避免 lateral braking

Factors Affecting COD & Perceptual–Cognitive Ability#

  • COD vs Agility 差異

    概念定義
    COD預先計畫好方向改變（pre-planned）
    Agility含 感知-決策反應（perception + action）

  • COD < 75 ° 時 觸地時間 <250ms 所以訓練上 生理：similar to speed training (concentric explosiveness); ＋認知感知訓練 COD > 75 ° 時 觸地時間 >250ms 所以訓練上 生理：增加 focus on eccentric strength & max strength

  • tests without a reactive aspect should be considered change-of-direction tests

  • tests such as the Illinois agility or L-run that require a bend to maintain velocity instead of an aggressive change of direction could be considered tests of maneuverability

  • Change of Direction ability

能力提升與下列有關

  • 轉向角度越大（如180°） → 需↑ braking impulse + lateral trunk tilt
  • 身體組成：↑ muscle mass, ↓ fat mass → ↑ T-test 表現
  • Lower center of mass: body center of mass height is related to improved lateral change-of-direction performance
  • Reacceleration：↑ hip extension velocity
  • Deceleration strength : eccentric strength
  • Posture & trunk control：防止滑倒、側偏
  • Neuromuscular: must be specifically trained using high-velocity eccentric contractions
  • Drop landing
  • Loaded jump landings
  • Power clean/snatch catch phase
  • 🧠 Perceptual–Cognitive
  • Visual scanning（掃描）
  • Pattern recognition（模式辨識）
  • Anticipation（預測）
  • Decision-making time & accuracy（反應決策）
  • Knowledge of situation（戰術經驗）
  • 決定 agility 真正表現差異的核心（比單純 COD 更重要）
  • Technical guideline and coaching
  • 眼睛 注意力放在對手 肩膀 軀幹 髖部
  • Body position
  • 減速時控制 trunk 不要大位移
  • trunk, hips 往要去的方向 轉向
  • low center of mass
  • Leg: avoid stiff-leg brake. Cue “push the ground away”
  • Arm: powerful arm

Methods of Developing Speed#

  • Sprinting : 提升跑步速度最重要的運動
  • 提升 RFD, impulse: weightlifting, jump w/ SSC
  • ↑ rate coding (high central nervous system activity) 方法：resisted and assisted sprint training
訓練方式／範例潛在優點潛在缺點實務建議
Assistance TrainingRope towing, bungee pull, downhill sprint↑ stride rate 可能促進神經適應 → ↑ max speed⚠️ stance phase 過短 → ↓ propulsive force ⚠️ 可能 ↓ muscle activation (出現 “chop steps” )⚠️ downhill 產生過度離心負荷🔺證據有限 ⚠️可能傷害 SSC 動作機制⚠️注意運動員狀態與技術成熟度✅ 若使用，需嚴格控制強度與距離
Resistance TrainingSled towing, wind resistance, incline sprint, sled push↑ acceleration phase 力學（低姿態：躯幹、髖、脛骨角度）↑ RFD, ↑ propulsive force → ↑ acceleration & top speed⚠️ 負重過高會 ↑ contact time, ↓ stride length ⚠️ 過斜坡度影響動作技術 ⚠️ sled push 去除 arm action → gait 改變✅ 身體狀況決定負重 ⛹️‍♂️田徑選手：負重不致↓速度 >10–12% 🏉field sport：可用 20–30% BW，強化初段加速（5–10m）
  • Strength 要專注在 Power：clean, snatch, med ball throw, mid thigh pull
  • Mobility ：freedom of an athlete’s limb to move through a desired range of motion

vs flexibility: joint’s total range of motion

  • flight position 常被 mobility 限制

Methods of Developing Agility#

COD vs Agility：訓練比較#

項目COD（封閉技能）Agility（開放技能）
測驗505, T-test, Pro AgilityReactive Y-drill, Light-board, verbal cue
重點能力Eccentric loading, impulse controlVisual scan, decision making, reaction time

  • Strength: 專注在 relative strength 跟 speed-strength (power) + eccentric 要 COD
  • squat jump, CMJ, drop jump + COD drills

🔄 Change of Direction (COD) Development#

  • progression
  • deceleration drills 前後 -> 側向 -> 減速後加速
  • cut 角度 < 75 -> >75°
  • shuffles
  • COD 慢速 (5m 加速 -> 10m加速)

🧠 Agility Development（含反應）#

  • COD drill + 感知 + 決策任務（stimulus → action）
  • 調整 cue（視覺/聲音）強度 + 隨機性
  • progress to small side games SSG, Evasion games

📈 Progression Model#

  • Technique emphasis（靜態練習與體態控制）
  • Pre-planned drills（封閉技能訓練）
  • Variable drills（混合情境）
  • Open drills with stimuli（實戰模擬）

Program Design#

變項#

  • exercise interval : 1rep 時間/距離
  • 順序
  • 頻率
  • 強度 : % max
  • Restry interval: rep 間 / 組間休息
  • Repetition(reps)
  • Sets: Reps + rest interval
  • Series: sets+recovery interval
  • Volume: 3 sets of 5 reps
  • Work-to-rest ratio
  • Volume load: volume * intensity(weight)

Speed program#

  • short-to-long method: 先練 acceleration phase -> 再提升 top velocity phase
  • Monitoring Sprint Ability 以前用 40 yard dash, 現在用儀器
  • ground contact time ↓ , step length (連續兩步)↑ , stride length (同一隻腳) ↑, speed, acceleration
  • Flight time ↑ (要配合觸地時間看 太長表示太多上下震)
  • stride angle

Agility Program#

  • 原則： periodization.(COD -> agility) 不能random or only sports specific (SSG)
  • Needs analysis -> assess COD, agility (包含認知感知), maneuverability
  • Monitoring Agility Ability
  • COD deficit: 跑直線跟COD一樣距離差異秒數 (505 vs 10m)
  • Ground contact time -> 看COD進步是 sprint or real COD
  • Exit velocity ↑ , Entry velocity, decision making time

🧠 指導原則 Coaching Strategies#

  • Emphasize quality > fatigue
  • 每組 drill 前給明確目標與 cue
  • 小組設定強度 → 預防過度疲勞影響表現與學習
  • Monitor signs of CNS fatigue：步頻下降、反應延遲、技術走樣

✅ High Yield Points#

  • Speed/agility 訓練應技能優先、疲勞控制
  • 加速 vs 極速 vs agility → 訓練策略各異，不可混淆
  • 排序邏輯：technical → explosive → fatigue-based
  • 長期計畫應搭配 periodization，並回頭檢驗 KPI 改善幅度
