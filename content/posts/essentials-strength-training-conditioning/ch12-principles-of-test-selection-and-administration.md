## Learning objectives
- identify and explain reasons for performing tests,
- understand testing terminology to communicate clearly with athletes and colleagues,
- evaluate a test’s validity and reliability,
- select appropriate tests, and
- administer test protocols properly and safely. The author would like to acknowledge the significant contribution of Everett Harman to this chapter.

## Reasons for Testing

- 🎯 **為何進行測驗？**

> Testing can be used to assess athletic talent, identify physical abilities and areas in need of improvement, set goals, and evaluate progress

- 評估選手體能（talent identification）
- 找出需改善能力（training focus）
- 設定目標（goal setting）
- 檢驗進步（progress tracking）
- 💡 Testing = 訓練規劃的客觀依據

## Testing Terminology
| 詞彙 | 定義 |
| --- | --- |
| **Test** | 評估特定能力的程序 |
| **Field test** | 不需實驗室、設備簡單（如跳遠、T-test） |
| **Measurement** | 收集測驗數據 |
| **Evaluation** | 解釋測驗結果，決定是否需調整訓練 |
| **Pretest** | 訓練前測基本能力 |
| **Midtest** | 訓練過程中追蹤進展 |
| **Formative evaluation** | 根據 midtest 修正課表 |
| **Posttest** | 訓練後判斷成效 |

## Evaluation of Test Quality（測驗品質評估）

### 🔍 兩大核心：

- **Validity（效度）** = 測驗能否測出它想測的東西（最重要）
> Validity is the degree to which a test or test item measures what it is supposed to measure; this is one of the most important characteristics of testing.

- **Reliability（信賴度）** = 測驗結果是否一再一致

### 效度類型：

| 類型 | 定義 | 範例 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Construct Validity** | 測驗是否真正反映某抽象概念（理論核心） | <span class="r">垂直跳測爆發力 power</span> |
| **Face Validity** | 表面看來是否合理（不等同於科學驗證） | 教練覺得 shuttle run 很像比賽情境 |
| **Content Validity** | 測驗是否涵蓋所有相關面向 | 體能測驗是否包含力量、有氧、敏捷等 |
| **Criterion-Referenced Validity** | 測驗結果與已驗證標準的相關性 | 一個新測驗與 VO₂max 之相關係數高 |

- Criterion-referenced validity

| 類型 | 定義 | 關鍵差異 | 範例 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Concurrent Validity** | 測驗結果是否與目前可接受的**金標準測驗**一致 | ✅ **同時比對** | 垂直跳 vs. 高科技力板同步測爆發力 |
| **Predictive Validity** | 測驗結果是否能**預測未來表現** | ✅ **未來導向** | 40-yard dash 測成績是否能預測 WR 表現 |
| **Discriminant Validity** | 測驗能否**區分不同能力或族群** | ✅ **能區分才有意義** | 一項肌力測驗不應與柔軟度高度相關 |

- {{< logseq/mark >}}**Concurrent validity** is the **extent** to which test scores are associated with **those of other accepted tests**{{< / logseq/mark >}} that measure the same ability
- **Convergent validity** is evidenced by **high positive correlation between results of the test being assessed and those of the recognized measure of the construct(the “gold standard”).**
- Convergent Validity	測驗與理論上相似的其他測驗結果一致性	通常透過**相關係數（r）**檢驗，r > 0.7 通常為佳 看是否“聚合”到同構念
- **Predictive validity** corresponds with future behavior or performance.
- **Discriminant validity** distinguish between two different constructs and is evidenced by a low correlation between the results of the test and those of tests of a different construct
- Concurrent = 現在 vs. 標準 → 「我這個測驗能否跟公認準的對上？」
- Predictive = 現在 vs. 未來 → 「我這測驗能否預測誰能成功？」
- Discriminant = 不該相關的果然無關 → 「我這測驗能否只測我想測的東西？」

### **Reliability（信賴度）= 穩定性**

- 測驗**是否能在不同時間、操作者、場地下得到一致結果？**
- ❗ 可靠 ≠ 有效（你可以**穩定測錯誤的東西**）

#### 📌 Test–Retest Reliability

Statistical correlation of the scores from two administrations

- 同一測驗在不同時間施測 → 結果應相近
- 測量信度的工具 **typical error of measurement (TE)**
equipment error + biological variation of athletes 包含

- 1. **Intrasubject variability** is a lack of consistent performance by the person being tested
- 2. **Objectivity（客觀性）** = **Interrater reliability**
- 不同測驗者對同一動作是否給出相同評分
- 3. **Intrarater variability** is the lack of consistent scores by a given tester 自己給分數不標準
- 4. Test 本身就無法 consistent

## Test Selection（選擇測驗原則）

- 💡測驗必須**符合比賽特性與運動員狀況**
- 原則：
- 能量系統匹配（如 300-yard shuttle 對應 anaerobic）
- 動作特異性（如垂直跳 vs. 踢腿）
- 技術難度與經驗（新手避免技術依賴高的測驗）
- 年齡 & 性別（如青春期測驗安排）
- Environmental factors such as temperature, humidity, and altitude

95° F = 35° C 85° F=30° C

### Aerobic Endurance Testing in the Heat

- **Using temperature limits at least 5°F (3 °C) below those listed is recommended, especially on sunny days, to provide a safety margin and enable better test performance.**
- acclimate at least a week from cold climate
- hydration 24hr prior
- Plain water is most appropriate for exercise up to 1 hour in duration.

## Test Administration（測驗安排）

| 項目 | 建議 |
| --- | --- |
| **測驗順序** | 技術/爆發→力量→耐力（避免疲勞影響） |
| **測驗條件控制** | 同樣時間、場地、天氣、溫度、hydration |
| **測驗人員** | 熟悉流程、避免鼓勵差異（影響信賴度） |
| **測前準備** | 標準暖身（general + specific）、先示範與說明 |
| **測後恢復** | 高心跳測驗（如300-yard shuttle）須 cooldown |

### Test sequence

- 1. Nonfatiguing tests (e.g., height, weight, flexibility, skinfold and girth measurements, vertical jump)
- **2. Agility tests (e.g., T-test, pro agility test)**
- 3. Maximum power and strength tests (e.g., 1RM power clean, 1RM squat)
- 4. Sprint tests (e.g., 40 m sprint with split times at10 m and 20 m)
- 5. Local muscular endurance tests (e.g., push-up test)
- 6. Fatiguing anaerobic capacity tests (e.g., 300-yard[275 m] shuttle)
- 7. Aerobic capacity tests (e.g., 1.5-mile [2.4 km] run or Yo-Yo intermittent recovery test)
