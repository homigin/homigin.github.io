## 🔥 Warm-Up

- 好處：分成
**Temperature-related effects** - ↑ muscle & core temperature, {{< logseq/mark >}}↑  neural function{{< / logseq/mark >}},  disruption of transient connective tissue bonds
**Non–temperature-related effects** - ↑  blood flow to muscles, {{< logseq/mark >}}↑  baseline O2 consumption{{< / logseq/mark >}},  {{< logseq/mark >}}postactivation potentiation{{< / logseq/mark >}}

- ⏱目的：↑肌收縮速度、力量、傳導速率、O₂供應 (Bohr effect)，↓黏滯阻力、傷害風險
心理準備

- > The structure of the warm-up influences potential improvements; as such, the warmup needs to be specific to the activity to be performed
- 包含2部分：全程 10-20mins. 活動前 15min內 完成，太早沒用。
- **General warm-up**：5–10 min 有氧(慢跑 跳繩): ↑HR、血流、肌溫、呼吸率 -> general stretching
- **Specific warm-up**：動態活動，模擬比賽動作（ex: 跳躍、動態伸展）
- 功能：動作準備、神經啟動、心肺刺激、心理集中

### Targeted and Structured Warm-Ups

建議結構：**RAMP protocol**

- **R**aise (general warmup)： ↑體溫、HR、呼吸、血流、關節活動
- **A**ctivate & **M**obilize (stretching)：啟動關鍵肌群（agonist, stabilizer），動態活動改善ROM
➡dynamic stretching and mobility exercises > static stretching

- **P**otentiate (specific warm-up)：比賽前神經激發（↑神經驅動），強度遞增模擬競賽要求
➡high-intensity dynamic exercises
平常 training 時這段就可以放 speed training and agility training

- ⚡與傳統warm-up相比，RAMP強調動作導向與個別化設計，↑效能

## 🧘 Flexibility

Flexibility = 關節活動範圍 (ROM) ➕ 肌腱肌肉延展性

- Static flexibility：關節被動可達之 ROM
- Dynamic flexibility：動態控制下活動範圍（functional ROM）
- Mobility: flexibility + balance, postural, perception
> flexibility viewed in isolation can be misleading, as normal ROM does not guarantee normal movement

影響因素：

- Joint structure 🦴：ball-and-socket > ellipsoidal > hinge
- Age & Sex：年紀↑ , ROM ↓；女生 > 男生（骨盆結構＋激素）
- Connective tissue：肌腱、韌帶、筋膜、肌肉彈性
>**Elasticity** (the ability to return to original resting length after a passive stretch)

- Resistance training：有助提升ROM，若full ROM進行
- Muscle bulk：過多會限制ROM
- Activity level：活動多者通常ROM佳
- regular stretching program can increase an athlete’s stretch tolerance, thus potentially permitting further increases in flexibility

### Freq, Duration:

- Stretching twice per week, for a minimum of five weeks, has been shown to significantly improve flexibility
- duration: static 15-30 secs
- All stretching sessions should be preceded by a period of general activity to raise muscle temperature.
- monitored for a loss of sensation or radiating pain

### When stretch?

- post-workout 5-10分鐘內
- 要增強 flexibility 需要 another session: 適合 recovery day. Warm-up -> stretch

### Proprioceptors and Stretching

- 慢慢伸展：避免刺激 muscle spindle產生stretch reflex
產生tension 刺激 Golgi Tendon Organ 讓肌肉放鬆
-> same muscle being stretched : autogenic inhibition 要拉的肌肉先主動 contraction
-> 放鬆時主動收縮 the opposite muscle : reciprocal inhibition

## 🔄 Types of Stretching

- **Active Stretching**：個體自身收縮對抗
- **Passive Stretching**：外力協助達ROM

**Static Stretching**：緩慢伸展至mild discomfort，維持15–30秒

- 安全性高、肌肉張力低，常用於冷身🧊
- **Ballistic Stretching**：動態彈跳拉伸，可能引發stretch reflex⚠️
- **Dynamic Stretching**：運動前使用，主動控制 ROM，模擬比賽動作

**PNF Stretching**（Proprioceptive Neuromuscular Facilitation）

- 涉及主動肌收縮與被動拉伸組合
- prestretch of 10 seconds

模式：

- hold-relax - isometric contraction against force 6 secs. -> relax -> passive stretch 2nd time 30s.
- contract-relax - 不只hold, 還往回推 concentric contraction > relax -> passive stretch 2nd time 30s.
**autogenic inhibition**

- hold-relax with agonist contraction -> 前面一樣，最後 moving further into the new ROM. 自己出力伸展。**reciprocal inhibition** + **autogenic inhibition**

- Chest (figure 14.13)

## STATIC STRETCHING TECHNIQUES

- 容易搞混組合比較表

| Stretch | 主拉肌群 | 功能與應用 | 常見錯誤 | 不建議使用時機 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Straight Arms Behind Back** | anterior deltoid, pec major, biceps | shoulder extension + chest opener🏋️‍♂️ 推類訓練前後拉伸 | 胸椎過度伸展代償肩關節活動不足 | 肩部急性疼痛時避免 |
| **Seated Lean-Back** | abdominals, anterior shoulder/chest (次要) | thoracic/lumbar spine extension🧘‍♀️ 適合用於 spine mobility 放鬆 | lower back collapse, hyperlordosis | pre-workout 時 spine 還沒熱時避免 |
| **Side Bend with Straight Arms** | latissimus dorsi, QL, obliques | overhead lateral flexion，模擬 overhead movement | 肩胛內收不足，手臂偏斜 | 肩夾擠症候群者 |
| **Side Bend with Bent Arm** | QL, obliques（較少 lat） | 更專注在 torso 屈側肌群💆‍♀️ 適合日常放鬆 | torso 前傾代償 lateral flexion | pre-throwing warm-up 少用（不模擬 overhead） |
| **Sitting Toe Touch** | hamstrings, erector spinae | hamstring 與 lower back 同時拉伸🛌 睡前、靜態柔軟性評估 | 過度脊椎屈曲壓迫椎間盤 | pre-workout（↓力量） |
| **Semi-Leg Straddle** | hamstrings（單側）, glutes | 單側控制更佳，減少spinal load🏃‍♂️ 跑者適合 | 無意識轉向造成側彎 | 無 |
| **Step Stretch** | gastrocnemius（直膝） | ankle dorsiflexion 提升，改善深蹲 | 後腳腳跟抬起→拉不到 | 無 |
| **Wall Stretch** | soleus（屈膝）+ gastrocnemius（部分） | 足底落地姿勢重建，適合跳躍運動 | 腳掌沒貼好 | 無 |

- 若運動前**必須用靜態伸展**（ex: 長跑前、團課前），建議選擇：
| 身體部位 | 建議 Stretch | 原因 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hip flexor | Forward Lunge | 防止過度前傾、協助 warm-up hip ROM |
| Hamstring | Semi-Leg Straddle | 單側控制佳，spine 更安全 |
| Shoulder | Straight Arms Behind Back | 不易代償，模擬 overhead prep |
| Calf | Step Stretch | 踝關節活動度對跑跳很重要 |

- 運動前應避免的 Static Stretch
| Stretch | 原因 |
| --- | --- |
| Sitting Toe Touch | ↓神經驅動 + ↑下背壓力 |
| Spinal Twist (Pretzel) | spine冷時風險↑ |
| Butterfly | 坐姿 passive stretch 可能造成骨盆代償 |
