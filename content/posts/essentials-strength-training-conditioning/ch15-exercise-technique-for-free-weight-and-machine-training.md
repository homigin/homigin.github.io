## Learning objectives
- understand the general techniques involved in properly performing resistance training exercises,
- provide breathing guidelines,
- determine the appropriateness of wearing a weight belt,
- provide recommendations for spotting free weight exercises, and
- teach proper resistance training exercise and spotting techniques.

## Fundamentals

### Grips

| Grip | 特徵 | 用途/範例 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pronated (overhand) | 掌心向下 | bench press, pull-up |
| Supinated (underhand) | 掌心向上 | chin-up, biceps curl |
| Alternated | 一手正握一手反握 | deadlift |
| Hook grip | 拇指夾住 bar 下方 | Olympic lifts |
| Neutral grip | 掌心相對 | hammer curl |

- **握距**分類：寬、中、窄 → 依動作需求決定

### Stable Body & Limb Positioning 🧍

- **穩定性原則**：腳穩定貼地 + barbell line of action ≈ 重心
- **地面站姿**：雙腳 shoulder-width，拇指朝前，膝蓋微彎

**椅上臥姿五點接觸**（Five-Point Contact）：

- 後頭部
- 上背與肩胛
- 臀部
- 左腳足底
- 右腳足底

### Range of Motion (ROM) and Speed

- 完整 ROM = 最大化肌肉發展與彈性，控制動作 → 避免使用動能（momentum）
- Speed: Controlled ≠ always slow
通常：eccentric 稍慢，concentric 爆發（但非 ballistic）

### Breathing & Valsalva

- > exhale through the sticking point
- **Exhale** during **concentric** → esp. sticking point
**Inhale** during **eccentric**
**例**：bench press ⏩ exhale halfway ↑、inhale ↓

**Valsalva maneuver**：

- transient（1–2 sec）閉氣 ☑️
- ↑ intra-ab pressure → ↑ spinal support
- 🚫副作用：HTN, dizziness, blackout
- 限於 trained lifter + structural lifts（e.g. 1RM squat）

### Weight Belt

- **使用時機**：大重量，腰部負擔大（如 squat, deadlift）
- **不需使用**：小重量或非腰部主力動作

## ✅ Spotting

- **必要情境**：bar over head/face/back/shoulder/clavicles

**重要原則**：

- Overhead bar on back/front: 要在 power rack. 放保護槓. 要淨空區域.
- over-face：liftoff 用 alternated grip，lift 中用 supinated grip
solid, wide base of support with the feet and a neutral spine position

- squat：從後方扶住胸椎兩側
- dumbbell spotting：手靠近手腕，而非肘部
spot as close to the dumbbells as possible

- Do Not Spot Power Exercises

## Exercise techniques

- Chest
- Chest Exercises 比較
| 動作 | 主訓練肌群 | 特點 | 適合目標 | 常見錯誤 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Flat BB Bench Press** | Pec major, ant deltoid, triceps | Compound, bar path固定，重量潛力最大 | 增加 maximal strength、測試 1RM | elbows flare、arch too much |
| **Flat DB Bench Press** | 同上 | 多自由度，活動度 ↑、穩定需求 ↑ | muscular balance、stabilizer activation | DB too wide/low |
| **Incline DB Bench Press** | Pec major (clavicular), ant deltoid | 上胸強調、肩關節負擔 ↑ | 改善上胸發展、美觀 | elbows too low or flare |
| **Flat DB Fly** | Pec major (isolation) | 單關節、伸展範圍大、泵感強 | 肌肥大、配合 bench press 輔助訓練 | bend elbows too much |
| **Vertical Chest Press (Machine)** | Pec major, ant deltoid, triceps | 固定軌道、安全、學習門檻低 | 初學者、rehab、warm-up | not adjusting seat height |
| **Pec Deck (Machine)** | Pec major (isolation) | 單關節 isolation、泵感佳 | 對集中收縮有幫助 | shoulder internally rotated |

| 功能需求 | 建議動作 |
| --- | --- |
| 💪 Max Strength | Flat BB Bench Press |
| 🔧 改善左右肌力不平衡 | Flat/Incline DB Press |
| 📐 上胸發展 | Incline DB Bench Press |
| 🧠 感受度訓練 | DB Fly, Pec Deck |
| 🧷 安全與穩定 | Vertical Chest Press |

- Incline DB press : spot: Grasp the athlete’s forearms near the wrists.
- DB fly: spot: one knee on the floor with the foot of the other leg forward and flat on the floor (or kneel on both knees)
- Hip & thigh

| 動作 | 主訓練肌群 | 特點 | 適合目標 | 常見錯誤 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Back Squat** | Glute max, hamstrings, quad | Compound, 高負重潛力 | 全身力量、下肢發展 | heel raise, valgus collapse |
| **Front Squat** | Quad ↑↑, glutes, core | 重心前移 → 膝主導、軀幹正直 | OL選手、quad dominant 發展 | elbows drop, round back |
| **Hip Sled (Leg Press)** | Quad, glutes | 穩定性高、可用大重量 | 初學者、安全性↑、hypertrophy | ROM不足、膝過伸 |
| **Step-Up** | Quad, glutes, hamstring | 功能性、單腳穩定度↑ | 運動表現、平衡、單腳力量 | 推蹬太用腳尖 |
| **Forward Lunge** | Glutes, quad, hamstring | 動態、穩定性需求高 | 運動員轉向、體能表現 | 前腳不穩定，膝超足尖 |
| **Good Morning** | Hamstrings, glutes, spinal erectors | Hip hinge pattern、後鍊 dominant | 增強 hip extension 與姿勢穩定 | 背圓、膝太伸死 |
| **Deadlift** | Glutes, hamstring, lumbar | 全身 compound、hip hinge dominant | 運動表現、後鍊發展 | 背圓、硬拉變蹲 |

| 目標 | 建議動作 |
| --- | --- |
| 🏋️ Max Strength (全身) | Back Squat (Low Bar), Deadlift |
| 🦵 Quad hypertrophy | Front Squat, Step-Up |
| 🍑 Glute focus | Low Bar Squat, RDL, Lunge |
| ⚖️ 單腳穩定/運動表現 | Step-Up, Lunge |
| 🚫 Lower back sensitive | Hip Sled, Goblet Squat |
| 🧘 Mobility 限制者 | Hip Sled, Partial ROM Squat |

- Hip sled: 下降到 thighs are parallel to the foot platform
- Do not allow the buttocks to lose contact with the seat, the hips to roll off the back pad, or the heels to rise off the foot platform
- do not forcefully lock out the knees
- Back squat: 下降到 until the tops of the thighs are parallel to the floor, the trunk begins to round or flex forward, or the heels rise off the floor
- High Bar vs. Low Bar 比較
| 比較項目 | High Bar | Low Bar |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 槓鈴位置 | trapezius 上方 | deltoid 後、spine of scapula |
| 重心路徑 | 更直立 | 稍微後傾、hip hinge 多 |
| 主動肌群 | quad ↑、glutes | glutes ↑、hamstring、背部 |
| 軀幹角度 | 較直立 | 前傾明顯 |
| 深蹲角度 | 較直（Olympic style） | 髖屈曲多（Powerlifting style） |
| 初學者適合 | ☑️ 比較好上手 | ❌ 需肩胸活動度與技巧 |
| 負重潛力 | 較低 | 通常較高 |

### Lunge / Step-Up

- **Knee tracking**：膝蓋應對齊第二腳趾，避免 valgus
- **Foot plant direction**：前腳朝正前方/略內轉（如前所述）
Plant the lead foot flat on the floor pointing straight ahead or slightly inward.

- **Step-up platform 高度**：膝關節彎曲角約 90°（太高易代償）
- Deadlift

| 動作 | 主肌群 | 特點 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Conventional | Glute, hamstring, spinal erector | compound、全身動作 |
| RDL | Hamstring, glute | hip hinge、膝少彎 |
| Stiff-Leg | Hamstring↑ | 膝 almost straight、拉伸感強 |

- RDL vs Stiff-Leg Deadlift

| 比較項目 | **RDL** | **Stiff-Leg Deadlift** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🦵 膝角度 | 輕微屈膝（soft knee） | 幾乎完全伸直（locked or near-locked） |
| 🔁 ROM 控制 | 控制至 mid-shin 或剛過膝 | 通常拉到底至腳踝（hamstring 拉伸極大） |
| 🧠 動作特徵 | hip hinge 主導 | hip hinge + hamstring stretch ↑ |
| 🔥 主動肌群 | hamstrings, glutes, erector spinae | hamstrings ↑↑, erector spinae |
| 💡 負重能力 | 較高（重訓友善） | 較低（柔軟度限制） |
| ⚠️ 受傷風險 | 背部壓力中等，控制良好風險低 | 背部張力↑，柔軟度不足易代償 |

- 📌 RDL
- 適合：肌力發展、臀腿訓練週期中期
- 應用：運動員 posterior chain 訓練、減少腰壓
- Cue：臀部向後推、bar 貼腿滑下、脊椎中立
- 📌 Stiff-Leg DL
適合：hamstring flexibility 測試、輕負荷輔助訓練

- 應用：目標拉伸感與 isolation 動作
- Cue：膝蓋鎖定、髖關節主導、慢控制下放
- Shoulder
- upright row: At the highest bar position, the elbows should be level with or slightly higher than the shoulders and wrists.

| 動作 | 主訓練肌群 | 類型 | 特點 | 常見錯誤 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Shoulder Press (Machine)** | Deltoid (anterior & middle), triceps | Compound / Machine | 穩定、安全、學習曲線低 | seat 太低 → ROM ↓ |
| **Seated Barbell Shoulder Press** | 同上 | Compound / Free Weight | 負重潛力大、核心參與 | 背過度拱、bar path 不直 |
| **Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press** | 同上 + stabilizers | Compound / Free Weight | ROM↑、平衡需求↑ | dumbbell 下放太低 |
| **Upright Row** | Deltoid, upper traps, biceps (短頭) | Compound / Vertical pull | medial deltoid 強化、對 trapezius 有刺激 | elbow 高於肩 → impingement risk |
| **Lateral Raise** | Middle deltoid | Isolation | shoulder hypertrophy 必備、可做 drop set | using momentum、shrugging |

| 功能需求 | 建議動作 |
| --- | --- |
| 💪 Max Strength | Seated BB Shoulder Press |
| 🧘 初學者/恢復 | Machine Shoulder Press |
| 🧠 Control / Feel | DB Press or Lateral Raise |
| 📐 Medial Deltoid hypertrophy | Lateral Raise, Upright Row（moderate） |
| 🚫 Shoulder Impingement 避免 | 限制 upright row ROM 或避免 |

- Triceps

| 動作 | 主訓練肌群 | 類型 | 特點 | 常見錯誤 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Lying Barbell Triceps Extension**(a.k.a. Skull Crusher) | Triceps brachii（長頭↑） | Isolation / Free Weight | ROM 大、張力持續、重訓經典 | elbows flare、bar 過頭 |

- Both exercises may be performed behind the neck using either a clean grip or a snatch grip.
- Dip: Continue the dip to a depth not to exceed a quarter squat, the catch position of the power clean, or 10% of the athlete’s height
