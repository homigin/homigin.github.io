## Factors Related to Aerobic Endurance Performance

**VO₂max**

- 最大有氧能力，↑ training status → ↑ VO₂max

ceiling level for endurance performance

- advance athlete 進步幅度有限，用 HIIT improve peak power output, ventilatory threshold, hydrogen ion buffering, and utilization of fat as a fuel source

**Lactate Threshold (LT)**

- %VO₂max at which [lactate]↑ abruptly

- **LT** > VO₂max for predicting performance 🚀

- ↑ LT → ↑ sustainable pace

- maximal lactate steady state = lactate 不堆積的運動強度，比 V.O2 max or LT 更好的表現預測指標

**Exercise Economy**

- O₂ cost @ submax pace

- **↑ Economy = 同樣 VO₂ 跑更快**

- 跟體型、mechanics、neuromuscular coordination 有關

- An improvement in exercise economy can enhance maximal aerobic capacity (V.O2 max) and lactate threshold

**Type I Muscle Fibers**

- ↑ oxidative enzymes, capillary density, mitochondria

- fatigue-resistant → ↑ endurance performance

## Designing an Aerobic Endurance Program

### Step 1: Exercise Mode（運動類型）

- Specificity‼️ training 要 mimic event demands（e.g. cycling vs running vs swimming）

### Step 2: Training Frequency（每週頻率）

- 建議：**>2x/wk** 才對 VO2max 有幫助

- ↑ freq → ↑ adaptations，但需考慮： training status, intensity & duration & recovery, sport season

- !! Off season - 5x/week, Preseason - 每天 or 一天多次！

- Maintain期 不需太多次

- \>5x/week ↑ 受傷機率

- 初學者 2–3x/wk
中階／進階 4–6x/wk（含交叉訓練

### Step 3: Training Intensity（訓練強度）

- Most accurate: O2 消耗 看 % VO2max & lactate 監測

- 決定 adaptation type：LT, VO₂max, speed

- HR monitoring（**Karvonen formula**）👇
**Target HR = [(最高HR – 休息HR(HRR)) × %intensity] + 休息HR**
**age-predicted (estimated) maximal heart rate (APMHR)** =220 - age

- RPE:

- One **metabolic equivalent (MET)** is equal to 3.5 ml/kg/min:  O2 consumption at rest

- 5.0 Walking, 4 miles per hour (6.4 km/h), level surface
10.5 Running, 6.7 miles per hour (10.8 km/h)

- Power measurement - for cycling

| 強度區間 | 訓練目的 |
| --- | --- |
| 60–70% VO₂max | LSD, recovery |
| 70–85% VO₂max | tempo, aerobic zone |
| >90% VO₂max | interval, HIIT |

### Step 4: Exercise Duration（每次時間）

- 取決於：強度（反比）→ intensity ↑，duration ↓

- goal（race length, base building）

| Training Type | 建議 Duration |
| --- | --- |
| LSD | ≥30–120 min |
| Tempo (85%) | 20–30 min |
| Intervals | 重複數組，每組30秒–5分鐘 |
| HIIT | 30秒–4分鐘/rep |

### Step 5: Exercise Progression（漸進設計）

- aerobic fitness does not decrease for up to five weeks when intensity of training is maintained and frequency decreases to as few as two times per week

- **唯一原則：逐步↑ volume OR intensity OR frequency，非同時**

- 每週至少一天 recovery or active rest day

- 建議：每週 ↑ frequency, intensity, or duration  <10%
3週 ↑ 1週 deload（避免 overtraining）
taper 前可逐步↓ volume，maintain intensity

## Types of Aerobic Endurance Training Programs

依據 intensity、duration、目標不同分為下列 5 種主要類型：
| 類型 | 強度 | 時間/間隔 | 目標 |每週次數|
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LSD | ~70% VO₂max | >比賽時間 | base, fat use, ↑ CV/mito | 2-3|
| Tempo | ~LT | 20–30 min | ↑ LT, race pace感覺 |2-3|
| Interval | >VO₂max | 30s–5min (1:1-3) | ↑ VO₂max, tolerance |1-2|
| HIIT | ~VO₂max | 30s–4min (2-3:2) | ↑ VO₂max, sprint-end power |1 only|
| Fartlek | varied | 20–60 min | mix 適應, mental engagement | 1 only|

### 1️⃣ Long, Slow Distance (LSD)

- Intensity：~70% VO₂max or 80% HRmax（RPE ~ 5）(Zone 2)

- Duration：**長於比賽距離或時間**

Goal：

- ↑ aerobic base
↑ mitochondrial density, capillarization, fat utilization
↑ CV function, thermoregulation
↑ lactate threshold intensity

- 缺點：speed 無法顯著提升, 沒有刺激 neurological recruitment

### 2️⃣ Pace/Tempo Training（Threshold Training）

- Intensity：At or slightly ↑ than lactate threshold, 「comfortably hard」(RPE 6,7) (Zone 3)

- 形式：steady or intermittent

- Steady（20–30 min）

- Intermittent (tempo/cruise interval)（e.g., 2×10min w/ 2 min recovery）

Goal：

- ↑ LT（跑更快而不累）

- ↑ running economy, mental toughness

- 關鍵：配速感 training、忍受 steady discomfort, sustain
If the workout seems relatively easy, it is better to increase the distance than to increase the intensity.

### 3️⃣ Interval Training

- Intensity：close to VO₂max（~90–100%）

- Work:Rest = 1:1 to 1:3（視目標）

- Duration（每組）：30s–5min (usually 3-5m)

- Goal：

- ↑ VO₂max

- ↑ anaerobic tolerance

- 注意：心肺負擔大，恢復須足夠💢

### 4️⃣ High-Intensity Interval Training (HIIT)

- Intensity：close to VO₂max（~90–100% HRR）加起來要 several mins 以上

- Interval：30s–4min；rest = equal or longer
6-10 x 2min / 5-8 x >3mins
An example of an appropriate work-to-rest ratio for long-interval HIIT training is ≥2 to 3 minutes at or above90% V.O2max, with relief bouts of ≤2 minutes (8, 9, 55)

Goal：

- ↑ VO₂max

- ↑ sprint-end power output

- ↑ anaerobic capacity

- ↑ running speed, economy

- 常用於 time-efficient training & advanced athletes

### 5️⃣ Fartlek Training（Speed Play）

- Intensity：**varied**（包括 LSD 70% & tempo 85-90%）

- Duration：20–60 min

- 結合自由變速（如上坡、下坡、節奏變化）

- Goal：↑ VO₂max、LT、running economy
增添趣味、心理變化控制

## Application of Program Design to Training Seasons

| Period | Goal |Freq| Training Features |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Off-season | ↑ base aerobic fitness |5-6| LSD, cross-training, technique, vol ↑ |
| Preseason | ↑ LT & race pace | 6-7|tempo, intervals, ↓ duration, ↑ intensity |
| In-season | 維持 performance | 5-6(練＋比賽)|race-specific runs, tapering, maintenance |
| Post-season | recovery / active rest | 3-5| cross-train, ↓ intensity & volume |

- increase in training duration should not be more than5% to 10% per week

- In-season: Low-intensity and short-duration training days should precede scheduled competitions so that the athlete is fully recovered and rested.

- Weekly Planning Considerations（Microcycle設計）:混合不同 training
ex: 1 LSD + 1 tempo + 1 interval + 1 recovery run

- Interference effect：避免 endurance & resistance 同日高強度（尤其腿部）

### 🗓️ 範例：Preseason Endurance Week Plan

| Day | Training Type |
| --- | --- |
| Mon | Rest / light cross-train |
| Tue | Interval (4×4min @ VO₂max) |
| Wed | Recovery run (~30 min @ easy pace) |
| Thu | Tempo run (~25 min @ LT) |
| Fri | Resistance training + technique drills |
| Sat | Long run (60–90 min @ LSD) |
| Sun | Cross-training / rest |

## Special Issues - cross-training, detraining, tapering, and resistance training

### Cross training:

在 reduced training 期間 （ injury / recovery from a training cycle）保持 general conditioning

- must be equal in intensity and duration to the athlete’s primary mode of exercise

- will not improve single-event performance to the same magnitude as mode-specific training only

- 太久沒訓練會 Detraining - cross training 可減少

### Tapering

- a typical tapering period may last between 7 and 28 days

- Linear taper - gradual change
Step taper - abrupt ↓ > 50% vol.
Progressive - abrupt ↓ 10-15%, gradual change each tier
Training volume is systematically reduced while intensity and frequency are maintained

### Resistance Training in Endurance Athletes

- ↑ injury recovery、↓ overuse injuries、↓肌力不均

- Hill running, final sprint、爆發力訓練皆受益

- 全身性肌力訓練能提升 endurance 表現

### Altitude

- Altitude is classified into several categories, ranging from sea level (>500 m) to low (>500-2,000 m), moderate (>2,000-3,000 m), high (>3,000-5,500 m), and extreme (>5,500 m)

- 海拔 > 1,200 m（~3,900 ft）→ VO₂max ↓, plasma vol ↓, HR ↑

- 適應期：Live high–train low（住高訓低）→ ↑ RBC mass（靠 EPO ↑）

- 回到平地後：效益可維持 2–3 wk

### 🎯 High Yield / Take-Home Points

- **LT、VO₂max、Running Economy** = endurance performance 三大關鍵

- **Specificity is king 👑**：event ≠ training → adaptation 低

- 頻率、強度、時間要 balance，過度提升＝過訓⚠️

- 強度用 HR zone 管理，Karvonen formula 是常考項目💥

- progression 應逐步設計，避免 simultaneously ↑ freq + intensity + duration

- 最佳訓練結果來自不同類型的混合運用（periodization）

- **LT training ≠ always max intensity；tempo 最貼近比賽需求**

- **Interval/HIIT** 是提升 VO₂max 的最快方式 💥

- LSD 適合 base building，但對 performance ↑ 限制

- LSD ≠ best for all；**interval + tempo** 更有效提升 LT, VO₂max

- Fartlek 為 mentally engaging 的 varied training，有趣又有效

- 每個 season 有不同 training goal，**不能一整年都做 LSD**

- Preseason 是 race-specific training 關鍵時期（tempo & interval）

- In-season：少即是多，focus on 維持而非 build

- **Taper = 保持強度、減少量 → 比賽日爆發💥**

- Post-season：避免 detraining，可利用 cross-train 恢復身體同時維持 CV fitness

- Resistance training 對 endurance athlete 有正面影響，不會干擾
