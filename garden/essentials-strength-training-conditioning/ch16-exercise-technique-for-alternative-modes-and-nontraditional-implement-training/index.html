Bodyweight Exercise#

  • 無需器材，適用於初學者、rehab、warm-up、家庭訓練 可用於 muscular endurance, strength, core control

✅ 優點（Benefits）#

Functional movement pattern（多為 closed-chain） 促進 stability（↑ proprioception） no need equipment 核心肌群 fire

⚠️ 限制（Limitations）#

負荷量難以調整（progression 受限） 高級訓練者易 plateau 難以單獨刺激特定肌群（如 isolation）

Closed-Chain 特性#

  • 四肢末端固定於地面或器械

  • ↑ 多關節協調，↓ 單關節代償

  • 較 open-chain（如 leg extension）更貼近運動動作模式

Core Stability and Balance Training Methods#

  • Core: axial skeleton and all the soft tissues with proximal attachments that originate on the axial skeleton

  • 核心穩定 = 動作效率 + 力量轉移平台

  • 防止 trunk flexion/extension/rotation 失控

  • 對於 running, lifting, throwing 等運動表現至關重要

  • Isolation exercises : dynamic or isometric muscle actions designed to isolate specific core musculature without the contribution of the lower and upper extremities

  • ex: plank, side plank

  • good for untrained and rehab. BUT not effective at improving sport performance

Ground-based free weight#

  • ex: squat, deadlift, overhead press, push press

  • 為 functional core training：核心參與力傳導而非單獨訓練

  • 優於 isolation core training（如 crunch）

  • Free weight ground-based exercises offer the ideal combination of specificity and instability, especially when one is focusing on strength and power development.

⚖️ Balance Training Devices (Instability Devices)#

Stability Ball、Wobble Board、Foam Pad、Suspension Trainer（如 TRX）

裝置特色用途
Bosu Ball半圓形單腳訓練、穩定挑戰
Balance Board不穩平台coordination、康復
Airex Padfoam pad軟墊，漸進平衡難度
Stability Ball大球增加 core 不穩定性

  • 🧠 Neuromuscular Activation

  • ↑ Core stabilizer but ↓ Prime mover force output（尤其大肌群） less than 70%

  • 適合 rehab、stabilization phase（early phase rehab, injury prevention） prevention ACL injuries 尤其術後

  • 🏃‍♂️ 運動應用（Application）

  • 運動員非比賽訓練階段可用作 activation

  • 進階訓練期 → 改回 ground-based, stable surface 力量訓練

  • 適合使用於 warm-up, rehab, balance integration

Variable Resistance Training Methods（可變阻力訓練法）#

類別裝置特性
🪝 鍊條（Chains）free-hanging steel chainsresistance ↑ 隨 barbell 上升
🧶 彈力帶（Bands）looped elastic bands彈性 ↑ 張力，適合加速區段負荷

  • Constant external resistance

  • traditional resistance training methods, represents real life activities

  • Accommodating resistance (semi-isokinetic)

  • speed of movement to be controlled throughout entire ROM

  • Poor external validity, unlikely to provide adequate training stimulus

  • Variable resistance

  • Combats the changing mechanical advantage maximize force application throughout the entire ROM

  • backsquat w/ bands - Take the other ends of the bands and place them over the ends of the bar so they rest against the outside of the weight plates on either side

🧠 Kinetics & Movement Effects#

Strength Curve Matching：

  • 運動越接近 lockout → 肌力自然 ↑

  • 鍊條/彈力帶使「負重 ↑ 配合肌力 ↑」

加速區段訓練（Accentuated Load）：

  • 適合 concentric phase 加速（如 push, squat）

神經招募 ↑：

  • 強化 stabilizer control，尤其在加速收縮中

  • 需良好 technique，避免 compensatory patterns

📈 Programming Considerations#

鏈條重量配置：

  • the absolute chain resistance at the top and that at the bottom portion of the movement are summed and then averaged.

  • 建議總重量中 5–10% 來自鏈條?

  • 鍊條接觸地面 → 穩定釋放重量，避免擺盪

彈力帶使用技巧：

  • using bands to substitute 35% of the total load during a back squat can acutely increase peak power by approximately 13%

  • 用鉤固定 barbell 兩端，避免側向位移

  • 起始點拉伸需一致，確保雙邊負重平衡

使用建議：

  • 適用於 strength-speed phase、爆發力開發週期

  • 不適用於新手或 rehab 階段

Nontraditional methods#

Strongman Training#

  • Tire flipping

  • tire 不該比 athlete 高

  • 窄胎對高個比較難，寬胎對矮個比較難

  • Three basic techniques:

  • Sumo: When tire is raised to hip or chest height, hands are rotated to allow forward pressing action with the arms

  • Back lift: deadlift stance ended with a forward-pressing motion

  • Shoulders against the tire: Preferred technique

  • Log Lifting

  • 類似做 clean , midrange grip to accommodate a pronated grip position

  • Can use %1RM from other lifts but may be more difficult to lift log

  • farmer’s walk develops total body anaerobic endurance

  • kettlebells are probably best used as general preparation exercise

  • 沒有傳統方法有效
