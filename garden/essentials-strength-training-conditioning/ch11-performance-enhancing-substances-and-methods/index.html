  • provide reliable and up-to-date information to athletes on the risks and benefits of performance-enhancing substances, including anabolic steroids;

  • understand the efficacy and adverse effects of over-the-counter dietary supplements marketed to athletes for enhancing sport and exercise performance;

  • determine which performance-enhancing supplements are beneficial for strength/ power performance, endurance performance, or both; and

  • distinguish between those performance-enhancing supplements that mimic the effects of hormones in the body and those that improve performance through some other means

  • The distinction between a drug and a dietary supplement is linked to FDA approval for safety and effectiveness.

  • Legal vs illegal ≠ safe vs unsafe

  • dietary supplements ≠ nutritional supplements 因為沒有營養成分

  • 維他命、礦物質、草藥、氨基酸、提取物

Hormonal Agents#

Anabolic Steroids#

✳️ Most common

  • 合成 testosterone 類似物 → ↑protein synthesis, muscle size, recovery

  • Stacking：使用多種藥物 ↑效能 the total dose of anabolic steroids has a logarithmic relationship to increases in lean body mass

  • Cycling：使用週期 → 減少副作用 5 to 10 weeks

  • Pyramid：先 titrate, 後 taper

  • Adverse effects：

  • Acne, aggression, ↓sperm, ↑BP, liver damage (17α-alkylated forms)

  • 👩：virilization（多毛、聲音低沉）

  • Psychological Effects: aggression, arousal, irritability

Testosterone Precursors（Prohormones）#

  • Examples：androstenedione, DHEA

  • ↓有效性、↑副作用 不太有效anobolic wise. However, androstenedione supplementation did cause an increase in serum concentrations of estradiol and estrone and was associated with lower high-density lipoprotein (HDL) levels.

HCG（Human Chorionic Gonadotropin）#

  • 男性使用（exogenous testosterone後）→ 刺激 testes 產生 endogenous testosterone

  • ⛔️ 不改善 performance

  • 🎯 用於恢復睪丸功能（如治療不孕）

  • 沒什麼副作用

Insulin#

  • increases protein synthesis

  • 💀 Hypoglycemia risk（尤其 fasting or post-exercise）

HGH（Human Growth Hormone）#

  • ↑lean mass, ↓fat mass

  • ↔️ strength performance 沒明顯提升

🧠 Side effects：

  • Acromegaly（顱骨、手腳粗大）、organ enlargement、glucose intolerance、HTN

Erythropoietin (EPO)#

  • ↑RBC, ↑O2 carrying capacity → ↑aerobic performance

  • 🩸 ↑Hct, ↑viscosity → ↑stroke, MI, DVT 風險

  • ⛔️ 高風險項目（especially endurance athletes）

β-Agonists#

  • e.g., clenbuterol

  • Originally for asthma；off-label 作為 ↑LBM, ↓fat 用藥 Athletes generally use clenbuterol in doses twice the recommended amounts administered for clinical purposes, in a cyclic fashion (three weeks on alternated with three weeks off, with a two-days-on, two-days-off cycle during the “on” week)

  • ⚠️ tachycardia, tremor, ↑BP

β-Blockers#

  • ↓anxiety, ↓HR → precision sports（e.g., shooting, archery）

  • ⛔️ May impair endurance, ↑fatigue

  • b-blockers may improve physiological adaptations from aerobic endurance training by causing an upregulation of b-receptors

Dietary Supplements 💊#

🔹Essential Amino Acids (EAA)#

  • Essential amino acids include isoleucine, leucine, valine, lysine, methionine, phenylalanine, threonine, and tryptophan.

PVT TIM HALL

  • BCAA 特別是 leucine → 觸發 muscle protein synthesis via the Akt/mechanistic target of rapamycin(mTOR) pathway

  • 用途：提升肌肉修復，與 resistance training 合用 ↑效益

  • arginine synthesis of protein and creatine - metabolism results in the production of nitric oxide but muscle blood flow is not increased in healthy individuals following arginine supplementation

🔹Branched-Chain Amino Acids (BCAA)#

  • 包括 leucine, isoleucine, valine

  • 主要在 skeletal muscle 代謝（非 liver）

  • 📌 Leucine 最重要 → ↑mTOR → ↑MPS（肌蛋白合成）

  • 研究支持：可能 ↓DOMS，支持恢復，但 全蛋白效果更佳

🔹 b-Hydroxy-b-methylbutyrate (HMB)#

derivative of the EAA leucine and its metabolite a-ketoisocaproic acid.

增加蛋白質合成 減少分解 by ubiquitin. 合法.

  • Wilson GJ, Wilson JM, Manninen AH. Effects of beta-hydroxy-beta-methylbutyrate (HMB) on exercise performance and body composition across varying levels of age, sex, and training experience: A review. Nutr Metab (Lond). 2008 Jan 3;5:1. doi: 10.1186/1743-7075-5-1. PMID: 18173841; PMCID: PMC2245953.

While various studies analyzed in this manuscript support the efficacy of HMB as an effective ergogenic aid for athletes that decreases DOMS, markers of muscle damage, and body fat, while increasing various markers of performance, including LBM and strength in resistance trained athletes, and OBLA and VO2 peak in endurance trained athletes, a number of studies analyzed did not support the efficacy of HMB supplementation.

There is compelling evidence that HMB supplementation may be useful for clinical muscle wasting conditions including AIDS, cancer, bed-rest, and during periods of caloric deficits. HMB also appears to be safe, and may improve various markers of health, including blood pressure and LDL-cholesterol. When supplementing with HMB, current evidence suggests that 1 g of HMB should be consumed 3 times per day, for a total of 3 g of HMB daily (or 38 mg/kg of bodyweight). However, more studies are needed to determine the optimal dosage and frequency of HMB supplementation, and the overall efficacy of HMB supplementation as an ergogenic aid for athletes.

Nutritional Muscle Buffers#

  • 肌肉緩衝能力（MBC, muscle buffering capacity）→ 抵抗酸化，↑運動表現

  • MBC 與以下有正相關：repeated sprint ability, anaerobic threshold, training volume, high-intensity capacity（e.g. soccer, sprint, hockey, cycling, rowing, triathlon）

  • The ability to regulate H + concentration in skeletal muscle during high-intensity exercise has been termed muscle buffering capacity (MBC)

β - Alanine#

  • 非必需aa，來源：肉、雞. 是 carnosine 的 rate-limiting precursor.

  • carnosine → 主要位於 fast-twitch fibers，佔總MBC最多40%, ↑carnosine → ↑MBC → 延遲肌肉酸化、↑無氧表現

  • 效益 : 不↑max strength、VO2 max❌ ↑anaerobic threshold✅（提高乳酸門檻 → endurance 可維持高強度更久）

  • 適用於：多回合、高酸性無氧運動（>60秒），如間歇跑、摔角、籃球

  • 劑量：2–6 g/day，分2–4次服用（避免一次大劑量）

  • 副作用：paresthesia（刺麻感）→ 高劑量單次服用易出現，1hr內消退

Sodium Bicarbonate#

  • ↑extracellular pH → 緩衝代謝性酸（esp. anaerobic activity）

  • 劑量：0.3 g/kg ~ 60–90 min pre-exercise

  • 提高 late-stage output（e.g. rowing後段衝刺改善）

  • 副作用：GI upset（可分次服用或搭配食物緩解）要先試過

Sodium Citrate#

  • 同樣機轉於 sodium bicarbonate，但 腸胃副作用較小

  • 效果 不一致（equivocal），部分研究報告顯著提升肌耐力（20%↑）

  • 劑量：~0.4–0.6 g/kg，於運動前60–90 min

L-Carnitine#

  • 理論上 ↑fat oxidation → endurance but 人體研究支持有限，效益不明確

  • 可能 ↓muscle damage（抗氧化）

  • 2 g/day for three weeks ↑ androgen receptors ↑ IGF binding proteins preserve IGF-I concentrations

🔹Creatine#

  • 📌 Gold Standard 補劑，最多研究支持

  • ↑PCr（phosphocreatine）儲存 → 快速供能（ATP-PCr system）

  • ↑strength, power, lean mass

  • 常見 loading protocol：loading：0.3 g/kg/day × 5 days (20-25g) maintenance：~2 g/day

  • Side effect：weight gain（肌肉＋水）

Stimulants#

🔹Caffeine ☕#

  • Central stimulant（↑alertness, ↓perceived exertion）

  • improve FA oxidation. ↑endurance, reaction time, strength（少部分）

  • 常見劑量：3–9 mg/kg ~ 60 min pre-exercise 300mg.

  • 高劑量副作用：GI upset, ↑HR, anxiety, insomnia

  • 禁藥等級（視比賽規定）

  • Redbull 等提神飲料

  • effective for increasing resistance training volume performance, other types of anaerobic exercise (including Wingate tests and speed/agility performance) are not as responsive to energy drink consumption

ephedrine#

  • b-agonis for asthma -> thermogenic

  • improve aerobic endurance performance

  • ephedrine + caffeine -> fat loss

  • 劑量：4 mg/kg caffeine + 0.8 mg/kg ephedrine

Citrus Aurantium 苦橙#

improvements in exercise time to fatigue (a measure of endurance performance)

🔹Citrulline Malate#

  • ↑arginine availability → ↑NO → vasodilation → ↑blood flow

  • 可 ↓muscle soreness, ↑work output（部分研究支持）

  • 劑量：~6–8 g pre-exercise

📌 High-Yield Takeaways#

  • Creatine：最有效、最安全，↑strength/power/lean mass✅

  • Caffeine：↑performance，但須考慮副作用與禁藥風險

  • Sodium bicarbonate：有用但易拉肚子💩

  • Citrulline & EAA/BCAA：恢復輔助，優於空腹訓練

  • 其他補劑（如 carnitine）→ 資料不一、效益有限

  • Anabolic steroid = most abused PED；搭配 stacking/cycling使用

  • EPO & insulin 危險性高，↑performance但致命風險

  • HGH 改善體組成但不一定↑strength

  • β-blockers、β-agonists為特定情境運動使用
